How LearnFlix works

A focused library for learning by video

Choose a category

Browse subjects such as programming, design, and study skills. Each course is tagged with a category and level.

Watch in the lesson player

LearnFlix can embed a public YouTube video or playlist. A permitted MP4 can also play from the project’s local media folder.

Curated, not hosted

This HTML, CSS, and JavaScript prototype organizes video sources. It has no upload server, account system, or private video storage.

Make a learner profile

Use the registration page to try the sample form. It demonstrates browser form fields and validation.
