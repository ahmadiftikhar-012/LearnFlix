A little about us

Learning should feel easier to begin.

LearnFlix is a student project exploring how a clear, welcoming interface can help learners find useful videos and build a steady study habit.

Our approach

Small steps add up.

The library groups educational videos by subject, then lets learners search and browse from one place. YouTube provides the online video stream; the site organizes lessons and does not host or upload them.
